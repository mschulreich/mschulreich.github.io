The bubble is shown at three epochs — when the Sun entered the Local Bubble, at the time of the most recent <sup>60</sup>Fe signal on Earth, and today — in purple shades that grow lighter toward the present. The orange curve traces the Sun’s trajectory over the past 12 Myr up to its present position, with the Sun marked in white at its entry into the Local Bubble and in yellow at the time of the <sup>60</sup>Fe signal. Cyan dots mark the present-day Sco–Cen members, and a cross the effective supernova source.'
